cat-clips

A small personal tool that assembles and uploads short cat videos.

cat-clips is a hobby project run by one person. It takes short cat clips that people have shared publicly, sets them to a licensed music track with a one-line caption, and uploads the result to its owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  1. Finds a short cat clip and downloads it.
  2. Writes a one-line on-screen caption for it.
  3. Assembles the clip, the caption and a music track into one vertical video.
  4. Uploads that video to the owner's YouTube channel on a fixed schedule.

Who can use it

Nobody else. There is no sign-up, no hosted service and no shared account. The software runs on its owner's own computer and signs in only as its owner.
