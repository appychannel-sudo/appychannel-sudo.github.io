CrowdSays

A small personal tool that assembles and uploads short videos.

CrowdSays is a hobby project run by one person. It takes a short video clip that people are already discussing publicly, adds narration and on-screen comment cards, and uploads the result to its owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  1. Finds a clip worth talking about and downloads it.
  2. Writes and voices a short spoken introduction.
  3. Assembles the narration, the clip and the comment cards into one vertical video.
  4. Uploads that video to the owner's YouTube channel on a fixed schedule.

Who can use it

Nobody else. There is no sign-up, no hosted service and no shared account. The software runs on its owner's own computer and signs in only as its owner.
